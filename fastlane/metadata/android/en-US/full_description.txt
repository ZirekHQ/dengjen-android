Dengjen is a text-to-speech engine for Kurmanji Kurdish. It adds Kurmanji to Android's system text-to-speech, so TalkBack, Select to Speak, e-book readers and any other app that speaks text can read Kurmanji aloud.

Speech is made on your phone with neural voices. The app uses the internet only to download a voice; after that it works offline and sends nothing anywhere.

<b>Getting started</b>
<ol>
<li>Open Dengjen and download a Kurmanji voice.</li>
<li>Tap "Open text-to-speech settings", choose Dengjen as the preferred engine and set the language to Kurdish.</li>
<li>TalkBack now reads Kurmanji with Dengjen.</li>
</ol>

Every screen works with TalkBack.
